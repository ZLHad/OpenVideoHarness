**Five new styles on swatch spec 2: a paper desk dossier, a lacquer screen, a visual investigation, a relief map, a dark 3D studio**
- Why: the first round of growing the library past 33. These five fill gaps the library had: real material on paper, a dark East Asian craft, an evidence-led news look, a map that measures real terrain, and a 3D route for the batch of 3D styles to come.
- `paper-desk-dossier` (纸桌档案, print-collage): a camera travels along a cream sheet on a walnut desk; two public-domain Watkins photographs, an index card that types itself, a stamp and a visible red pen land with their weight. 9 s, so the slide along the sheet takes 1.2 s and stays legible. Landscape and portrait.
- `lacquer-screen` (漆金螺钿屏风, traditional-craft): a six-panel black lacquer screen unfolds in perspective, a gold-tipped brush writes one line across the folds, mother-of-pearl set in at the turns, one vermilion seal.
- `visual-investigation` (视觉调查, news-documentary): a scanned night floor plan rises into wireframe as the camera tilts; a running timecode, an event track and numbered callouts with time and source. The event is invented and neutral. Landscape and portrait.
- `map-explainer` (立体地图解说, map-data): a generated shaded relief (no real place, no borders), the camera pushing in and tilting, a route growing leg by leg with heights read off the terrain, the title in a legend box. Landscape and portrait.
- `dark-studio-3d` (暗棚实物 3D, 3d-light): a steel gear, bearing and nut, path traced in Blender (Cycles) at their real size: the parts mirror each other and the softboxes, soft contact shadows, a 105 mm lens at f/4 whose focus follows the action, a kicker drawing the teeth out of the dark; the assembly explodes and drops back with weight, and a spec card (text in the scene, at the focus distance) gives the mass worked out from the model's volume. About 1.5 hours to render on the CPU (cost: Blender). A three.js version was made first and compared on the same frames; its reflections, contact shadow and depth of field were approximations.
- `styles/_swatch/render.sh` copies the pinned three.js r186 build (`engines/canvas-film/three/build`) into every stage as `three/`, so a swatch can be 3D without its own copy; `styles/_swatch/README.md` says how to import it and when Blender is the better route.
- `bin/vh style check --text` works on a Blender scene: it times the scene's `TEXTS` (`python3 swatch.py --texts`) with readcheck and says that textcheck cannot see text rendered as geometry (it used to stop at "could not stage the swatch").
- `tools/ci.sh`'s style fixture no longer copies the real styles' swatch lines of `tools/media.txt` (with real swatches registered, its library check named each as "names no style").
- The site lists the five with their thumbnails; the style counts are 38. The full swatches are registered in `tools/media.txt` for the release `styles-media` (not uploaded).
- Checked: each passes `bin/vh style check <slug> --text` and `--determinism` (1 against 3 workers, 240/240 frames identical, both aspects where there are two).
