**Five new styles drawn while they explain: whiteboard, chalkboard, gold line on dark, era media relay, deep-space explainer**
- Why: the library is to grow past a hundred styles; this is the first batch of the round under swatch spec 2. Paper explainers (type 06) had no preset, and the explainer-drawing family had only blueprint, dark math and iso anatomy.
- `whiteboard` (白板速写): a dry-erase marker draws on one long board at a hand's speed, colour changes only while the marker is lifted, the title is handwritten, and the camera slides along the board to clean space to loop. `chalkboard` (黑板粉笔): chalk grain on mottled slate; a felt eraser scrubs out a wrong guess and leaves its blurred ghost and dust, the right curve is chalked over it, and a sliding double board closes. `gold-line-dark` (暗底金线描): one warm-gold line of light draws a night scene (ridge, shore, boat, five moon phases on one arc), the title is laid like gold leaf under a gleam, and the line burns out in the order it was drawn. `era-media-relay` (时代媒介接力): the same swallow redrawn by brush, woodblock, CRT beam and pixels, each handover the new tool at work, and a water drop blooming back into rice paper; the music keeps its key while its instruments change era. `deep-space-explainer` (深空语义科普, family 3D 与光): light is gold, matter coral, observers blue for the whole film, a corner key says so once; a ray-marched black hole whose disk is lensed over and under its shadow, gold rays bending round it, one followed to a shader Earth where a blue ring answers.
- Each has `STYLE.md`, `tokens.json` (family, video types, tags, study, `swatch` spec 2, card), `swatch.js`, `score.json`, `events.json`, its own synthesised foley (`styles/_swatch/custom_sfx.py`, seeded; see below), a landscape and a portrait version (9–10 s), and a poster and 2 s preview in git; the full swatches are registered in `tools/media.txt` for the `styles-media` release.
- `styles/_swatch/vendor/hand.js`: hand-drawn strokes shared by the four drawing styles — paths resampled with a little wobble, drawn along a minimum-jerk speed profile (slow off the mark, fast in the middle, slow into the end) that also sets line width, the pen head, and the stroke sounds; plus pen flights, glyph-by-glyph writing passes, strokes that burn out from their start, and a running-average smear for fast camera moves.
- `styles/_swatch/foley.mjs` can now read the `FOLEY` of a scene that imports `../../vendor/` or `../../components/` the way the stage lays them out (it imports such a scene from a throwaway copy in that layout); before, it failed on every scene that used a shared library. `tools/ci.sh`'s style-media check builds its made-up repo with `tools/media.txt` minus the real styles' swatches: with any style registered there, the check failed.
- A style's own synthesised foley is build output now: `styles/*/sfx/*.wav` is in `.gitignore`, `render.sh` rebuilds the style's sounds (`custom_sfx.py <slug>`) before it mixes the foley, and `custom_sfx.py` computes every sound whatever is asked for, writing only the ones asked for, so one style's rebuild cannot shift another's random draws (all 49 rebuilt byte-identical from a clean copy; a re-rendered whiteboard swatch has the same sha256). The 14 WAVs committed before stay tracked until their styles are rendered again.
- The homepage's style wall: a spec-2 style's tile plays its `preview.mp4` (its full sample is not in git), and its lightbox plays the release asset through `<source type="video/mp4">` once `STYLES_MEDIA_UPLOADED` is set (the preview until then); `RELEASE_SAMPLES` names the assets and `tools/readme.py check` holds it to `tools/media.txt`.
- The style counts in the READMEs, the overview diagrams, `CITATION.cff` and the homepage are 38; the homepage lists the five with their thumbnails.
- Checked: `bin/vh style check <slug> --text` and `--determinism` (1 against 3 workers, every frame identical) for each of the five; `tools/ci.sh`.
