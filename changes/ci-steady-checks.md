**The motion-blur resume check no longer depends on how fast the machine is**
- Why: "render, no --motion-blur, over a blurred chunk a stopped render kept" and its `--motion-blur 1` twin stopped a blurred render with `--frame-timeout 2`, counting on its first chunk to be finished by then. On a slow CI macOS runner it was not (#197, a lockfile bump, failed on it and passed on a re-run), so the render kept nothing and the check had nothing to test.
- The blurred render now runs with a long `--frame-timeout` and is stopped by SIGTERM, as a Ctrl-C would stop it, as soon as its first chunk is on disk. The page still hangs on frame 3, so exactly one chunk is kept on any machine. The check also asserts that one chunk was kept and, when it fails, says what was kept and what the stopped render printed.
- The `bin/vh activity --follow` check waited `sleep 1` and `sleep 1.2` and assumed the follower had caught up. It now waits for the follower's first lines and for the review's end line.
