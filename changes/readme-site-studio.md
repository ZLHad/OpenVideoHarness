**Studio near the top of the READMEs and the homepage: its short film, what it is for, in its own pictures**
- Why: Studio, the window built on the skills and the command line, is one of the project's main features, and the READMEs mentioned it in a row of the tools table while the homepage had no word of it.
- The READMEs (both languages): Studio's 17-second film right under the opening wall, as a looping GIF, with a link to the version with sound on the homepage; Studio first in "What makes it different"; a section "Studio" before "What it makes", with four pictures and the logic in a few lines: see where each film has got to, point at the picture and write a note, hand a few notes to the agent at once, decide a stop on cards, and talk to the agent in the right-hand column. Quick start says the installer puts OVH Studio in your applications and gives the two ways in (click OVH Studio, or open Claude Code or Codex in the folder); "Open Studio" is shorter; the tools table, "Effort, and who decides" and the doc map point to it.
- The homepage: a section "Studio" right after the opening, in the top bar, with its film (muted on a loop where there is a mouse, from the start with sound on a click), the four things it is for, its three themes (Darkfield, Prism, Bridge) and how to open it; every picture enlarges on a click. "Get started" names OVH Studio, "How it works" and the capability map have Studio in them, and the style library no longer says every sample is 5 seconds of the same content (the newer ones are not).
- The pictures (`docs/assets/studio/`, one per language, WebP, about 1.1 MB in all) are Studio's own pages on a made-up demo of the repo's showcase films, drawn as its window draws them. `tools/readme.py` compares the two READMEs' `.en.webp` and `.zh.webp` pictures as one, as it does for svg, png and jpg.
- The film's files are on the release `media` (`studio-loop.gif`, `studio-film.mp4`, `studio-poster.jpg`); the homepage plays the mp4 from `docs/site/media/studio-film.mp4` in a clone and from the release otherwise, `tools/media.txt` lists it so `tools/fetch_media.sh` fetches it, and the poster is `docs/site/img/studio.jpg`.
