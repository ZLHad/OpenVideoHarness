# STORYBOARD — "Clawd and the Leaf" (12.0 s, 24 fps, bpm 120, silent)

Logline: Clawd wants to film a falling leaf with a tiny hand-cranked movie camera, but the wind snatches it every time Clawd frames the shot, so Clawd gives up and peers into the lens — and the leaf lands on Clawd's own head, right in the viewfinder.
World: one autumn hillside under a maple. Paper-cream ground, apricot sky, rust/orange maple crown, olive-ochre meadow, a pumpkin patch further along. Colour arc: soft morning gold (A) → breezier, a touch of lilac in the sky and cream wind streaks (B) → warm amber backlight in the viewfinder (C).
Motif: the viewfinder frame (rounded rectangle + corner brackets). It opens the film on the leaf and closes it on Clawd wearing the leaf. The golden leaf is the only saturated yellow in the film, so the eye always finds it.
Clawd's arc: hopeful → excited → focused → surprised → determined → sneaky (mischief) → angry → suspicious (of the camera) → notices → delighted.
Audio: none. bpm 120 (BEAT 0.5 s) drives idles; hits on beats where it helps.
